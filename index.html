A higher threshold turns more of the image white; a lower detail level smooths out fine texture and noise, and also keeps traced lines simpler. Converting can take a few seconds and may briefly freeze the page on a large or busy photo.

Playback

Drawing order

Off keeps your Illustrator layer order — best when that order is intentional. On, shapes redraw in whichever sequence keeps the "pen" moving the least between them; each shape still draws in its own natural direction. Applies to live playback, video export and G-code alike.

Ending

0s 5s
1.0s hold

How long the finished drawing is held before a loop restarts or an export stops — and what happens right after. "Fade out" and "Un-draw" make the ending loop back to blank seamlessly, which is what an exported video will do too.

Export format

"Auto" matches the canvas as shown on screen. A fixed ratio adds padding around the artwork so the exported video comes out exactly that shape — 9:16 for Stories/Reels, 1:1 or 4:5 for the feed, 16:9 for widescreen.

Video export

Records the animation in real time as MP4 when supported, otherwise WebM. Once it's done, a "Video ready" banner lets you save or share it with one tap.

Batch export

Exports every queued file, one after another, using the current Ending, Export format, Overlay and Pen tip settings. Each video downloads straight to your browser's downloads folder — it may ask once to allow multiple downloads.

Plotter export (G-code)

Width mm

Generic G-code for pen plotters (grbl-style machines): millimeters, absolute positioning, pen up/down on the Z axis. Check the Z heights and feed rate in the file's header comments against your own machine before running it. Only actual lines are plotted — flat fill shapes are skipped, since a physical pen can't fill an area.
